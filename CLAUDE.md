## Project overview

**Name:** d8tec.com — company website
**Domain:** Web
**Stage:** Alpha
**Primary languages:** TypeScript, TSX, CSS

D8 is a full-spectrum technology company operating across R&D, 
hardware/software integration, web and app development, and AI/data 
solutions. This website is the public face of the company — the place 
where potential clients, partners, and candidates come to understand 
who D8 is and what D8 does.
